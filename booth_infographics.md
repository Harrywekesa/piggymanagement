# Booth Infographic Concepts for the *pig* Project

## Design Philosophy
- **Premium Glassmorphism** – dark gradient background with frosted‑glass cards and soft shadows.
- **Typography** – clean sans‑serif (Inter or Outfit).
- **Palette** – deep teal/violet or midnight‑blue/purple with vibrant cyan, magenta, teal, orange accents.
- **Layout** – vertical flow, 3‑4 rounded translucent cards, each with an icon and headline.

## Production Tips
1. Export as **300 dpi PNG** or PDF for large‑format prints.
2. Include a **3 mm bleed** margin for vinyl/fabric printing.
3. Use file naming like `booth_infographic_<theme>.png`.
4. For printing, choose matte vinyl or UV‑coated fabric to keep the glass‑morphic look vivid.
