Digital Pig Farm Manager
An offline-first Android mobile application for smart pig husbandry, breeding management, health tracking, market linkage, feed formulation & financial analytics.

Kitale Agricultural Society of Kenya (ASK) Show 2026  ·  Exhibition Stand

Application Modules & Live Interface Features
MODULE 01
Herd & Lifecycle Registry
Track every pig's tag number, breed, biological stage (piglet, weaner, grower, finisher, boar, sow), lineage mapping, and weight gain history over time.
Individual Pig Profiles Lineage Mapping Weight History
MODULE 02
Breeding & Gestation Tracker
Log sow heat checks, boar servicing, and artificial insemination. Auto-computes expected farrowing date (114 days) with live gestation countdown and sow ranking.
114-Day Gestation Countdown Sow Performance Ranking Farrowing Logs
MODULE 03
Veterinary & Health Logger
Log health events for a single pig, a specific category, or the full herd. Captures disease diagnostics, age in weeks, treatment cost, and vaccination protocols.
Single / Group / Full Herd Disease Diagnostics Gilt & Vaccination Records
MODULE 04
Feed Formulator & Inventory
Formulate least-cost feed rations with exact kg/g ingredient weights. Tracks stock levels and automatically computes Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) and ADG growth rate.
FCR = Feed Consumed ÷ Weight Gained ADG Growth Rate Low Stock Alerts
MODULE 05
Market Connection & Buyer Log
Record buyer details (name, contact, butcheries/processors) directly at the point of sale. Automatically builds customer revenue history and market transaction logs.
Point-of-Sale Capture Butcheries & Processors Revenue Analytics
MODULE 06
Intelligent Alerts Engine
Automated alerts for imminent farrowing, vaccination schedules, heat checks, low feed stock, drug withdrawal periods, and market-ready pig notifications.
Farrowing & Heat Alerts Drug Withdrawal Block Market Weight Notice
MODULE 07
Reports Hub & Financial Analytics
Generate Profit & Loss reports, herd production summaries, pig mortality rates, and farrowing efficiency — filtered by Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, YTD, or All Time.
Profit & Loss (P&L) Pig Mortality Summary Breeding Stats
100% Works Offline
