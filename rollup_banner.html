Digital Pig Farm Manager
An offline-first Android mobile application for smart pig husbandry, health tracking, market linkage, feed formulation & financial analytics.

Kitale Agricultural Society of Kenya (ASK) Show 2026  ·  Exhibition Stand

Key Application Features & Screenshots
01
Herd & Lifecycle Registry Pig Profiles
Track every pig's tag number, breed, biological stage (piglet, weaner, grower, finisher, boar, sow), lineage mapping, and weight gain history over time.
Individual Pig Profiles Lineage Mapping Weight History
02
Veterinary & Health Logger Gilt Servicing
Log health events for a single pig, a specific category (e.g. all piglets/weaners), or the full herd. Captures disease name, pig age in weeks, treatment cost, and gilt servicing.
Single / Group / Full Herd Disease Diagnostics Gilt & Vaccination Records
03
Market Connection & Buyer Log Direct Sales
Record buyer details — name, contact, and buyer type (butchery, processor, live-market trader) — directly at the point of pig sale. Builds a full transaction and customer history over time.
Buyer Captured at Point of Sale Butcheries & Processors Full Revenue & Customer History
04
Feed Formulator & Inventory FCR & ADG
Formulate least-cost pig feed rations with exact kg ingredient distribution. Tracks commercial premix stock levels and automatically computes Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) and Average Daily Gain (ADG).
FCR = Feed Consumed ÷ Weight Gained ADG = kg/day Growth Rate Low Stock Alerts
05
Reports Hub & Financial Analytics P&L
Generate Profit & Loss reports, herd production summaries, pig mortality rates, and farrowing efficiency — filtered by Today, Last 7 Days, Last 30 Days, Year to Date, or All Time.
Profit & Loss (P&L) Pig Mortality Summary Breeding & Farrowing Stats
100% Works Offline
Direct Market & Buyer Linkage
FCR & ADG Pig Growth Analytics
